## About

I build AI systems that run in the real world: computer vision models optimized for edge hardware, the backends that serve them, and the interfaces people actually use. My work spans TÜBİTAK-supported medical imaging research, defense-industry detection and tracking, and industrial inspection.

**Currently**
- Full Stack Computer Engineer at **SSTEK**, building modular applications that integrate AI and computer vision
- Co-founder of **Menaco Software**, an AI & software startup incubated at Teknopark Ankara (Nar Incubation Center)
- Co-founder of **Tosoco**, focused on business automation, custom CRM systems and AI agent workflows
- Research: MS (Multiple Sclerosis) diagnosis from MRI with segmentation and classification, supported by TÜBİTAK
